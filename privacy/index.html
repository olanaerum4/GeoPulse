Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

GeoWawe is designed to collect as little data as possible. You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name or email.

Data stored on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage to remember your best scores, your theme choice (light/dark) and a temporary copy of country data so the game loads faster. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Visitor statistics

We use GoatCounter to count visits and game events (for example "game started" or "score 5–9"). GoatCounter does not use cookies, does not track you across websites and does not store personal data. It records aggregated information such as page, referring website, browser, screen size and approximate country. Our hosting provider (Netlify) also keeps standard server logs.

Third-party services

To show the game, your browser loads resources from:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) for the typeface,
  • flagcdn.com for flag images,
  • restcountries.com for country data,
  • gc.zgo.at for the GoatCounter script.

These services receive your IP address when your browser connects to them, as with any website, and handle it under their own privacy policies.

Advertising

GeoWawe does not currently show advertising. If ads are added in the future, this policy will be updated, and where required you will be asked for consent before any advertising cookies are used.

Children

GeoWawe is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
