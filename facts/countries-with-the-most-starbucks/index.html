☕ The 5 countries with the most Starbucks stores

Updated November 2026 · 3 min read

Starbucks has tens of thousands of stores worldwide, but they are spread very unevenly. Number three surprises most people.

The top 5

  1. #1🇺🇸United States≈16,500 stores

    The US has more Starbucks stores than the next four countries on this list combined.

  2. #2🇨🇳China≈7,600 stores

    China is Starbucks' fastest-growing big market, with thousands of stores opened in about a decade.

  3. #3🇰🇷South Korea≈2,000 stores

    South Korea has one of the highest numbers of Starbucks stores per person in the world, especially around Seoul.

  4. #4🇯🇵Japan≈1,900 stores

    Japan was Starbucks' first market outside North America, in 1996.

  5. #5🇨🇦Canada≈1,450 stores

    Canada has a large Starbucks presence, even alongside its own coffee chains.

Where Starbucks struggled

In 2008 Starbucks closed most of its stores in Australia, where people preferred local cafés and their flat whites. In Italy, the home of espresso, the first Starbucks only opened in 2018.
