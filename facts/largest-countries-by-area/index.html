🗺️ The 5 largest countries in the world by area

Updated November 2026 · 3 min read

Five countries together cover close to a third of all the land on Earth. Some of the comparisons between them are closer than most people expect.

The top 5

  1. #1🇷🇺Russia≈17.1 million km²

    Russia covers roughly an eighth of the world's land area. Its surface is larger than that of the dwarf planet Pluto.

  2. #2🇨🇦Canada≈10.0 million km²

    Canada is the second-largest country and has more lakes than any other country.

  3. #3🇺🇸United States≈9.8 million km²

    Whether the US or China comes third depends on how coastal and inland water is counted. Including water areas, the US is usually ranked ahead.

  4. #4🇨🇳China≈9.6 million km²

    China's land area alone is slightly larger than that of the US, which is why some lists swap places three and four.

  5. #5🇧🇷Brazil≈8.5 million km²

    Brazil is bigger than the contiguous United States and takes up almost half of South America.

Why maps fool us

The common Mercator map makes countries near the poles look far bigger than they are. Greenland looks as large as Africa on many maps, but Africa is about 14 times larger. Russia and Canada look enormous on maps too, and they really are huge, though not as huge as Mercator suggests.
