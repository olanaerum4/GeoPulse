🧳 The 5 most visited countries in the world

Updated November 2026 · 3 min read

Paris, Barcelona, New York, Rome and Istanbul all pull in huge crowds. These are the countries that welcome the most international visitors.

The top 5

  1. #1🇫🇷France≈100 million visitors

    France has been the world's most visited country for decades. It receives more foreign visitors each year than it has residents.

  2. #2🇪🇸Spain≈85 million

    Spain's beaches, islands and cities attract almost twice as many visitors as the country has inhabitants.

  3. #3🇺🇸United States≈66 million

    The US earns more money from tourism than any other country, even though it gets fewer visitors than France.

  4. #4🇮🇹Italy≈57 million

    Italy has some of the most visited cities on Earth, including Rome, Venice and Florence.

  5. #5🇹🇷Türkiye≈55 million

    Türkiye has grown quickly as a destination, with Istanbul and the Mediterranean coast leading the way.

How tourists are counted

These numbers count international arrivals, so one person visiting twice counts twice, and travellers crossing a border for a day trip may also be included.
