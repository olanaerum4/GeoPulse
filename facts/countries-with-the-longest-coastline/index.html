🌊 The 5 countries with the longest coastline

Updated November 2026 · 3 min read

One country completely dominates this list. Canada's coastline is longer than the next four countries' coastlines combined.

The top 5

  1. #1🇨🇦Canada≈202,080 km

    Canada borders three oceans, and its Arctic islands add a huge amount of shoreline. The total could wrap around the Earth about five times.

  2. #2🇮🇩Indonesia≈54,716 km

    With thousands of islands, Indonesia has the longest coastline in Asia.

  3. #3🇷🇺Russia≈37,653 km

    Much of Russia's coast faces the Arctic Ocean and is frozen for a large part of the year.

  4. #4🇵🇭Philippines≈36,289 km

    More than 7,000 islands give the Philippines an enormous coastline for its size.

  5. #5🇯🇵Japan≈29,751 km

    Japan's four main islands and thousands of smaller ones are surrounded by coast.

The coastline paradox

The length of a coastline depends on how closely you measure it. The smaller your ruler, the more twists and inlets you capture, and the longer the coast becomes. That is why Norway's coast is listed at around 25,000 km by the CIA, but at over 100,000 km by a detailed Norwegian measurement that includes every island.
