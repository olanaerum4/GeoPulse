👥 The 5 most populous countries in the world

Updated November 2026 · 3 min read

For centuries China was the most populous country on Earth. That changed in 2023, when India took the top spot according to UN estimates.

The top 5

  1. #1🇮🇳India≈1.45 billion

    India passed China in 2023 and is still growing, although more slowly than before.

  2. #2🇨🇳China≈1.41 billion

    China's population started to shrink in 2022, the first decline in decades.

  3. #3🇺🇸United States≈340 million

    The US is the third most populous country, with growth driven largely by immigration.

  4. #4🇮🇩Indonesia≈283 million

    Spread across thousands of islands, Indonesia is the world's most populous Muslim-majority country.

  5. #5🇵🇰Pakistan≈251 million

    Pakistan has one of the fastest-growing large populations and a very young median age.

The country to watch: Nigeria

Nigeria is sixth with around 230 million people. It is growing fast and is projected to become one of the most populous countries in the world later this century.
