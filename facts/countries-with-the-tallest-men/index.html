📏 The 5 countries with the tallest men

Updated November 2026 · 3 min read

The Netherlands is famous for its tall people, but the rest of the top five includes a few countries most people would never guess.

The top 5

  1. #1🇳🇱Netherlands≈183.8 cm

    Dutch men grew around 20 cm taller in about 150 years, thanks to better nutrition and health.

  2. #2🇲🇪Montenegro≈183.3 cm

    This small Balkan country on the Adriatic coast is almost level with the Netherlands.

  3. #3🇪🇪Estonia≈182.8 cm

    Estonia and its Baltic neighbours rank among the tallest nations in Europe.

  4. #4🇧🇦Bosnia and Herzegovina≈182.5 cm

    The Dinaric Alps region of the Balkans is known for some of the tallest people in the world.

  5. #5🇮🇸Iceland≈182.1 cm

    Iceland completes a top five dominated by Europe.

Why are some countries taller?

Genes play a role, but height also depends on childhood nutrition, health care and living conditions. That is why average heights in many countries rose sharply during the 20th century.
