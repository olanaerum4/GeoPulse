🕰️ The 5 countries with the most time zones

Updated November 2026 · 3 min read

Russia is the biggest country on Earth, so it must have the most time zones, right? Not quite. Once overseas territories are counted, France takes first place.

The top 5

  1. #1🇫🇷France12 time zones

    Thanks to territories in the Caribbean, the Indian Ocean and the Pacific, such as Guadeloupe, Réunion and French Polynesia, France spans 12 time zones. Counting its claim in Antarctica, it is 13.

  2. #2🇷🇺Russia11 time zones

    Russia has the most time zones in one connected piece of land. When it is evening in Kaliningrad, it is already the next morning in Kamchatka.

  3. #3🇺🇸United States11 time zones

    The 50 states use six time zones. Territories such as Guam, Puerto Rico and American Samoa add five more.

  4. #4🇬🇧United Kingdom9 time zones

    Britain itself uses just one time zone, but its overseas territories, from the Falkland Islands to Pitcairn, stretch across the globe.

  5. #5🇦🇺Australia9 time zones

    Mainland Australia has three main time zones, and several of them shift by 30 minutes rather than a full hour. External territories bring the total higher.

The opposite extreme: China

China is about as wide as the contiguous United States, which would naturally span around five time zones. Yet the whole country officially runs on Beijing time. In the far west, the sun can rise as late as 10 in the morning.
