☕ The 5 countries that drink the most coffee per person

Updated November 2026 · 3 min read

Espresso is Italian and most coffee is grown in Brazil, but per person, the biggest coffee drinkers live in the far north of Europe.

The top 5

  1. #1🇫🇮Finland≈12 kg per person/year

    Finns are the world's biggest coffee drinkers. Coffee breaks are a fixed part of the working day, and many workplaces have them written into their agreements.

  2. #2🇳🇴Norway≈9.9 kg

    Norwegians drink filter coffee from morning to evening, often black and often in large mugs.

  3. #3🇮🇸Iceland≈9 kg

    Long, dark winters may help explain why Iceland's coffee culture is so strong.

  4. #4🇩🇰Denmark≈8.7 kg

    Coffee is central to Danish hygge, the idea of cosy time together.

  5. #5🇳🇱Netherlands≈8.4 kg

    The Dutch were among the first to bring coffee to Europe in the 1600s through their trading empire.

What about Italy and the US?

Italians drink lots of small espressos, which use less coffee per cup. Per person they consume roughly half as much as Finns. Americans drink around 4 to 5 kg per person per year.
