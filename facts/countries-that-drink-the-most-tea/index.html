🍵 The 5 countries that drink the most tea per person

Updated November 2026 · 3 min read

Afternoon tea is a British icon, but the UK is only third. The world's biggest tea drinkers per person live in Türkiye.

The top 5

  1. #1🇹🇷Türkiye≈3.2 kg per person/year

    Strong black tea served in small tulip-shaped glasses is offered everywhere, from homes to shops. Most of it is grown along the Black Sea coast.

  2. #2🇮🇪Ireland≈2.2 kg

    The Irish drink more tea per person than the British, usually strong and with milk.

  3. #3🇬🇧United Kingdom≈1.9 kg

    Tea is still the UK's national drink, even if coffee has been catching up.

  4. #4🇮🇷Iran≈1.5 kg

    Tea is served with almost every social occasion in Iran, often with sugar cubes held between the teeth.

  5. #5🇷🇺Russia≈1.4 kg

    Russian tea culture is built around the samovar, a large heated urn for boiling water.

Growers vs drinkers

China and India produce most of the world's tea and drink huge amounts in total. Because their populations are so large, however, they rank lower per person.
