🏝️ The 5 countries with the most islands

Updated November 2026 · 3 min read

Ask people which country has the most islands and most will guess Indonesia or the Philippines. The real answer is much colder: the top three are all in the Nordics.

The top 5

  1. #1🇸🇪Sweden≈267,570 islands

    Statistics Sweden counted 267,570 islands, including tiny rocky skerries along the Baltic coast and in its many lakes. Fewer than about 1,000 of them have permanent residents.

  2. #2🇳🇴Norway≈239,000 islands

    Norway's long, fjord-cut coastline is lined with islands, from the Lofoten archipelago to the far north. The Norwegian Mapping Authority's count is just over 239,000.

  3. #3🇫🇮Finland≈179,000 islands

    Many of Finland's islands sit inside its lakes. The country is often called the land of a thousand lakes, but the real number of lakes is closer to 188,000.

  4. #4🇨🇦Canada≈52,000 islands

    Canada's Arctic archipelago includes some of the largest islands on Earth, such as Baffin Island, Victoria Island and Ellesmere Island.

  5. #5🇺🇸United States≈18,600 islands

    From Alaska's Aleutian chain to Hawaii and the Florida Keys, the US has thousands of islands spread across two oceans.

Why do the counts vary so much?

There is no single definition of an island. Some surveys count every rock that sticks out of the water, others only count islands above a certain size. Countries with detailed national surveys, like Sweden and Norway, end up with enormous numbers. Indonesia is usually listed with around 17,000 islands, and it has far more inhabited islands than Sweden.
