## Tips
- **Best times:** 17:00–21:00 local time on weekdays, and late morning on weekends. Test and look at your analytics after a week.
- **Reply to comments with videos.** "@user said Canada has more McDonald's…" → make the next video about it.
- **Cross-post** the same files to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
- **Double-check the facts before posting.** The numbers are rounded estimates from public sources (2023–2024).
- When a video takes off, make **part 2** in the same style quickly: "Part 2 because you asked 👀".
