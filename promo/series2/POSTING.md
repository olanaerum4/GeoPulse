## Tips
- Add a **fast, high-energy trending sound** at 10–20% volume; the built-in kick drums are on the cuts.
- Pin a comment: "What should the next Top 5 be? 👇". Then make the most-requested one.
- Double-check numbers before posting; some lists vary by source (comment wars are fine, real errors are not).
