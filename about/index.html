🌊 About GeoWawe

GeoWawe is a free, fast geography game that tests how well you really know the world, and usually shows you that the world is stranger than you think.

Why GeoWawe?

Most geography quizzes ask you to memorise capitals. GeoWawe asks something more fun: which is bigger? Which country has more people, more islands, more coffee drinkers or more McDonald's? It turns out our instincts are often wrong. Sweden has more islands than Indonesia, France has more time zones than Russia, and Finland drinks twice as much coffee per person as Italy.

Every answer comes with a short fact, so you learn something whether you win or lose.

Who makes it?

GeoWawe is an independent project. It has no investors and no sign-up, and it is built to work well on phones. New categories, facts and game modes are added regularly, often based on ideas from players.

Where the data comes from

  • Countries, capitals, regions, area and borders: REST Countries and the open world-countries dataset.
  • Population: UN World Population Prospects 2024 estimates for major countries; older figures adjusted for growth for smaller countries.
  • Life expectancy, GDP, CO₂ and similar: rounded figures from the UN, World Bank and similar public sources.
  • Fun facts (McDonald's, coffee, islands and so on): rounded estimates from recent public figures, company reports and national statistics.
  • Flags: flag images from flagcdn.com.

Numbers are rounded and sources sometimes disagree. If you spot something that looks wrong, please let us know and we'll check it.
