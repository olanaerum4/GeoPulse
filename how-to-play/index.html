🎮 How to play GeoWawe

GeoWawe is a fast geography game about comparing countries. Answer correctly to build a streak. One wrong answer and the game is over.

The four game modes

⬆⬇ Higher or Lower

You see one country's value, for example its population. Guess whether the next country's value is higher or lower. If you're right, the second country becomes the first one in the next round, so the chain keeps going.

🍔 Fun Facts

Quirky comparisons such as "Where are there more McDonald's?", "Who drinks more coffee?" or "Where are there more metal bands per person?". Tap the country you think wins. Watch out: sometimes the question asks for fewer or less.

⚔️ This or That

Two countries and one category. Tap the one with the higher value. From level 3, some questions ask for the lower value instead.

🚩 Flag Quiz

One flag, four countries. Pick the right one. From level 3, the wrong answers come from the same region, so you can't rely on the continent alone.

Levels and the timer

Every 5 correct answers takes you up a level. Each level gives you less time and closer comparisons.

LevelNameTime per question
1Rookie18 s

Flag Quiz gives you 4 seconds less per question (minimum 6 seconds).

Levels 1 and 2 use around 50 well-known countries. From level 3, all 194 independent countries can appear. The timer pauses automatically if you switch tabs or apps.

Categories

Population, land area, population density, life expectancy, median age, GDP per person, coastline, CO₂ per person, number of neighbouring countries, and fun facts such as McDonald's, Starbucks, coffee, beer, chocolate, tea, pasta, Nobel Prizes, islands, average height, UNESCO World Heritage Sites, billionaires, tourists and heavy metal bands.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • ↑ / ↓: Higher / Lower
  • ← / →: left / right country in This or That and Fun Facts
  • 1–4: answers in Flag Quiz
  • Enter: play again · Esc: back to home

Tips for long streaks

  • Think about size first. Big populations usually mean more McDonald's, more billionaires and more tourists, but not always.
  • Per person changes everything. Small rich countries often win "per person" categories, such as coffee in Finland.
  • Watch the question. "More" and "fewer" questions are mixed in at higher levels.
  • Read the fact after each answer. The same countries come back, and the facts help next time.

FAQ

Is GeoWawe free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no download. It runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers.

Can I install it as an app?

Yes. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, choose Install app from the browser menu. It then opens full screen and also works offline.

Where does the data come from?

Country data comes from public sources such as the UN, the World Bank, the CIA World Factbook and REST Countries. Fun-fact figures are rounded estimates from recent public figures. See About for details.
